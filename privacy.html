How this application uses data

This policy describes Five Brand Creator Automation, a personal-use Windows desktop application operated by Vineet Kumar for his own creator channels. It covers the local application and this informational website.

Data accessed through Google and YouTube

After the account owner grants OAuth permission, the application may access channel IDs, channel names, channel statistics, video IDs, video metadata, privacy and scheduling settings, thumbnails, and other YouTube account data needed for the owner's requested task. It receives OAuth access and refresh tokens. Uploading a video or thumbnail also sends the selected local media and metadata to YouTube. The application does not collect Google passwords.

Purpose and control

Data is used to identify the correct channel and carry out account-owner instructions, such as reading information, uploading content, updating metadata, setting a thumbnail, scheduling a video, or deleting a specifically selected video. The tool checks the expected channel ID before saving account access or acting on content.

Storage and retention

OAuth credentials are encrypted using Windows DPAPI in the creator's local Windows environment. Local reports can contain channel IDs, names, statistics, and verification times. Tokens and reports remain until the owner removes them, revokes access, or replaces the local setup. Video and image files remain in the locations selected by the owner unless the owner separately requests file removal.

Sharing and AI assistance

Requests and selected media are sent to Google's YouTube API to perform the authorized features. Relevant API results may be processed in the owner's chosen Codex or AI-assisted working environment when the owner requests assistance. That provider's handling of data is governed by the owner's account settings and its applicable privacy terms. The local application does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or use it to train generalized AI models.

Use and transfer of Google API data follow the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Data is used for the user-facing channel-management features described here.

Revocation and deletion

The owner can revoke access at Google Account connections. The owner can also remove the locally encrypted credentials and reports through their Windows/Codex environment, or contact the app operator below for help. Removing local app data does not delete videos on YouTube. A separate authorized video-deletion operation deletes the selected YouTube video and may be irreversible.

Website hosting

This informational website does not contain advertising, analytics scripts, or an account sign-up form. The selected hosting provider may process standard request information, such as IP addresses and technical logs, under its own privacy policy. No YouTube tokens, channel reports, or private media are hosted on this informational website.

Third-party services

The application uses YouTube API Services. Google's handling of information is described in the Google Privacy Policy. YouTube use is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service. If Codex is used, see OpenAI's privacy policy and the settings for the owner's account.
